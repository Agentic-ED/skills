# The Memory — Knowledge Management Guild

> **Mascot:** elephant · a group of elephants is called *memory*.

## What we cover
Capturing and finding what an organization knows: knowledge bases, taxonomies, SME interviews, and content lifecycle.

## What belongs here
A skill belongs in `knowledge-management/` when its main user is someone doing knowledge management work and its output is something they would ship or use in that role. If a skill serves several guilds, put it where its primary user lives and link to it from the others.

## Our principles
1. **Practitioner-first.** Skills come from real work and are tested on real requests before they merge.
2. **Honest outputs.** Our skills never invent facts, citations, standards, statistics, or data — they say what they don't know.
3. **Accessible by default.** Plain language, inclusive examples, and accessible formats.
4. **Small and sharp.** One skill does one job well; long reference material lives in `references/`.
