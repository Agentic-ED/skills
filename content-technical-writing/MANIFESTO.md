# The Bouquet — Content & Technical Writing Guild

> **Mascot:** pheasant · a group of pheasants is called *bouquet*.

## What we cover
Clear learning and product content: job aids, how-to guides, documentation, style guides, and plain-language editing.

## What belongs here
A skill belongs in `content-technical-writing/` when its main user is someone doing content & technical writing work and its output is something they would ship or use in that role. If a skill serves several guilds, put it where its primary user lives and link to it from the others.

## Our principles
1. **Practitioner-first.** Skills come from real work and are tested on real requests before they merge.
2. **Honest outputs.** Our skills never invent facts, citations, standards, statistics, or data — they say what they don't know.
3. **Accessible by default.** Plain language, inclusive examples, and accessible formats.
4. **Small and sharp.** One skill does one job well; long reference material lives in `references/`.
