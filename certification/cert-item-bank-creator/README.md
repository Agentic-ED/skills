# Certification Item Bank Creator

**Guild:** [Certification — The Parliament 🦉](../MANIFESTO.md)

Drafts certification exam items from your exam blueprint and source material — with answer keys, rationales for every option, cognitive level, and source references — ready for subject-matter-expert review.

## Who it's for
Certification program managers, psychometricians, exam developers, and item writers building or expanding an item bank.

## What you give it
- Exam blueprint or objectives (it can draft a provisional one if you don't have one)
- Source material the items must be grounded in
- Minimally qualified candidate profile, item types, and how many items

## What you get
- An item plan mapped to the blueprint (objective × cognitive level)
- Draft items with key, distractor rationales, source citations, and SME questions
- Coverage and key-distribution summary
- Markdown table or CSV for import

## Try it
> "Using this blueprint and the attached admin guide, draft 10 Apply-level multiple-choice items for Domain 2."

> "Expand our item bank for objective 3.1 with 5 scenario-based items; output CSV."

## Works well with
- [`sme-question-reviewer`](../sme-question-reviewer/) — review the drafts
- [`exam-benchmark`](../exam-benchmark/) — check the finished form and item statistics

## Limits
Drafts only. Every item still needs SME review and, ideally, pilot testing before it counts toward a score. The skill won't invent product facts; it flags items that need SME input.
