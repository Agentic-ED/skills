# The Parliament — Certification Guild

> **Mascot:** owl · a group of owls is called *parliament*.

## What we cover
Credentialing and certification exams: job task analysis, blueprints, item writing, SME review, standard setting, and exam quality.

## What belongs here
A skill belongs in `certification/` when its main user is someone doing certification work and its output is something they would ship or use in that role. If a skill serves several guilds, put it where its primary user lives and link to it from the others.

## Our principles
1. **Practitioner-first.** Skills come from real work and are tested on real requests before they merge.
2. **Honest outputs.** Our skills never invent facts, citations, standards, statistics, or data — they say what they don't know.
3. **Accessible by default.** Plain language, inclusive examples, and accessible formats.
4. **Small and sharp.** One skill does one job well; long reference material lives in `references/`.
