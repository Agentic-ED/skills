# SME Question Reviewer

**Guild:** [Certification — The Parliament 🦉](../MANIFESTO.md)

Reviews exam items the way an item-review panel would — subject-matter accuracy, blueprint alignment, item-writing flaws, and fairness — and gives every item an **Accept / Revise / Reject** verdict with a concrete rewrite.

## Who it's for
SMEs, exam developers, and certification managers preparing items for review meetings or publication.

## What you give it
- The items (table, CSV, or pasted text) with keys
- Optional but recommended: blueprint, minimally qualified candidate profile, and source material

## What you get
- Summary of verdicts and the most common flaws
- Per-item review with quoted issues and suggested rewrites
- Questions to raise with your human SMEs
- Set-level checks: key distribution, cueing between items, duplicates

## Try it
> "Review these 20 items against our blueprint and the attached product guide. Flag anything an SME panel would reject."

> "QA this practice exam for item-writing flaws and bias before we publish."

## Works well with
- [`cert-item-bank-creator`](../cert-item-bank-creator/) — draft items to review
- [`exam-benchmark`](../exam-benchmark/) — analyze how items perform after piloting

## Limits
A first-pass reviewer, not a replacement for your SME panel. Without source material, accuracy judgments are marked "unverified".
